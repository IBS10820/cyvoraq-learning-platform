# CYVORAQ Learning Platform

Responsive Master/Mentor and Student portals connected to Supabase-ready authentication, records and real-time messaging.

## Portals

- Master portal: `index.html`
- Student portal: `student.html`

## Included

- Student creation only under User Accounts
- Auto roll numbers and temporary passwords
- Active, paused and cancelled statuses
- Student progress, attempts, hours and certificates
- Progress-report printing inside each student profile
- Fees, failed-payment blocking and mentor override
- 3, 6 and 12 month 12-character vouchers
- Trial balance, cash flow, receivables, payables and balance sheet
- Compact Ask AI and Chat with Mentor buttons
- WhatsApp-style real-time mentor messaging
- Messages retained for offline users and shown after login
- Student-controlled personal profile fields
- Responsive desktop, tablet and mobile design

## Data protection

The system follows data-minimisation and storage-limitation principles. Student personal data should not be retained forever. Financial records are separated and retained according to applicable UK legal requirements and the organisation's documented retention schedule.

Accounting statements are management reports, not automatic HMRC or Companies House submissions. They must be reviewed and filed through authorised services or compatible software.
